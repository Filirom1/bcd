# Reports

Reports help you manage the library: track overdue items, identify high-demand titles, and carry out systematic weeding. This page is aimed at **BCD teacher-librarians**.

---

## Available reports

| Report | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Overdue** | Items not returned on time, grouped by class |
| **Most borrowed** | Most popular titles — supports reacquisition decisions |
| **CREW - Weeding** | Systematic weeding candidates |
| **Never borrowed** | Items with zero circulation |
| **Holds** | Active reservation queue |
| **Active loans** | All items currently on loan |

## Most Borrowed Report

Ranks **titles** (bibliographic records) by number of checkouts over a selected period. Use it both to identify successes and to make reacquisition decisions.

### Analysis methods

| Method | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| **All** | All borrowed titles, ranked by popularity |
| **Most borrowed** | Raw ranking by checkout count |
| **Turnover rate** | ISO 11620: checkouts ÷ copies — identifies under-stocked titles |
| **Scarce + high demand** | Titles with ≤ 2 copies AND high turnover → priority purchase |

### Period

Filter by **week / month / year / all time** to observe seasonal trends or recent successes.

### Table columns

| Column | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| **#** | Rank in the list |
| **Title** | Clickable → catalog record |
| **Checkouts** | Proportional blue bar — the longer the bar, the more the title circulates |
| **Copies** | Number of copies in the collection — red if only 1 copy |
| **Turnover rate** | Checkouts ÷ copies. Colour bar: green (< 4), orange (4–8), red (≥ 8) |

### Interpreting the turnover rate

The turnover rate is the ISO 11620 collection performance indicator. It is calculated as: **total checkouts ÷ number of items**.

| Rate | Interpretation | Recommended action |
|------|---------------|--------------------|
| **< 1** | Collection barely used | Strong weeding or collection realignment signal |
| **1 to 3** | Low utilisation | Analyse by segment (albums, fiction, nonfiction separately) |
| **3 to 9** | Normal usage | Compare year over year |
| **> 9** | Demand exceeds supply | Purchase additional copies |

> **Note:** A low overall turnover rate can mask a thriving fiction section and a dead nonfiction section. Use the filter panels to compute turnover by medium type.

### Statistical filter panels

Panels (histograms + range sliders) let you cross-filter the data:
- **By medium type**: albums, novels, nonfiction, comics, etc.
- **By genre**: adventure, fantasy, etc.
- **By audience**: child, youth, adult
- **Turnover rate**: slider to filter a range (e.g. show only titles with rate > 8)
- **Publication year**: colour-coded histogram — red (> 20 years), orange (10–20 years), blue (< 10 years)

### Show / hide columns or panels

The method bar has two buttons on the right:

- **⊞ (table icon)** — choose which columns to display in the results table
- **⊟ (columns icon)** — choose which statistical panels to display

Your choices are saved for future visits.

## CREW — Weeding Report

Weeding is the **systematic removal of deteriorated, outdated, or unused items**. A well-weeded BCD circulates better: students find things more easily, shelves breathe, and the budget focuses on genuinely useful acquisitions.

> **Practical rule:** aim to weed approximately **5% of the collection each year**, cycling through the full collection over 20 years.

### Available CREW methods

**1. Never Borrowed**
Items with zero checkouts since acquisition. The most revealing starting method: why has this book never been borrowed? Unattractive cover, poor placement, irrelevant topic?

**2. Low Circulation**
Items checked out 2 times or fewer over the last 2 years. Frees up space before new purchases.

**3. Damaged + Old**
Items in poor condition AND in the collection for 3+ years. Top priority: physical condition and age both accumulate score points.

**4. High Score (≥ 5)**
Overview of all urgent candidates, all criteria combined. Good starting point for a first weeding session.

**5. Never Inventoried**
Items never physically verified (minimum age: 1 year). Identifies potentially lost or misplaced items.

**6. Duplicate Low Demand**
Titles with 3+ copies AND low demand (< 2 checkouts/copy over 2 years). Keep 1–2 copies, weed the rest.

### The CREW score

Each item receives an automatic score accumulating several criteria:

| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| In collection for 1 year | +1 |
| In collection for 2 years | +2 |
| In collection for 3+ years | +3 |
| Damaged condition | +2 |
| Nonfiction published > 5 years ago | +1 |
| Nonfiction published > 10 years ago | +2 |
| Never borrowed | +2 |
| Borrowed only once | +1 |

| Score | Colour | Decision |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 0–2 | 🟢 Green | Keep |
| 3–4 | 🟠 Orange | Inspect physically |
| ≥ 5 | 🔴 Red | Priority weeding candidate |

> **The score is a guide, not a verdict.** A high-scoring but popular book should be replaced with a new copy rather than discarded. Always inspect physically before deciding.

### CREW thresholds by document type

The thresholds below follow the CREW manual (Texas State Library) and Réseau Canopé / SavoirsCDI guidance.

#### Fiction (novels, picture books, comics)

For **children's fiction**, publication age is not a primary weeding trigger. What matters: circulation history and physical condition.


**Exceptions — keep despite low circulation:**
- Titles on the Éduscol reference reading lists (cycles 2 and 3)
- Recognised literary prizes (Prix Sorcières, Pépite, etc.)
- Collection classics (Babar, Le Petit Prince, etc.)

#### Nonfiction — age thresholds by Dewey domain

Nonfiction accessible to 6–11 year-olds must be **factually accurate**. An outdated documentary claiming "the USSR is the world's leading power" or omitting Pluto from the solar system actively misleads students.

| Domain | Maximum age before review |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Computing / IT (004) | 2–3 years |
| Medicine, health (610) | 5 years |
| Geography, countries | 5–10 years (depending on geopolitical changes) |
| Sciences (500) | 10 years (except biographies and foundational theory) |
| Social sciences, economics | 10 years |
| Arts | Physical condition takes priority — less strict on age |
| History | Less strict — verify factual accuracy |
| Dictionaries, grammar | 15 years |

### MUSTIE criteria

During physical inspection, apply the MUSTIE criteria:

| Letter | Meaning | Practical example |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| **M** | **Misleading** | Factually wrong or dangerously outdated information |
| **U** | **Ugly** | Torn pages, detached cover, stains, mould |
| **S** | **Superseded** | Replaced by a newer or better edition |
| **T** | **Trivial** | No educational merit, superficial content |
| **I** | **Irrelevant** | Does not match the collection's audience or pedagogical project |
| **E** | **Elsewhere** | Available elsewhere and not needed in this collection |

A single MUSTIE criterion is sufficient to justify removal, regardless of score.

### Table columns and filters

**Available columns** (toggle ⊞ top right):
- **Score**: red/orange/green badge with detailed reasons
- **Barcode**: item identifier
- **Title**: clickable to catalog record
- **Condition**: good / damaged
- **Shelf location**: where to find it
- **Age in collection**: years on the shelf (red if > 3 years)
- **Publication year**: red if > 20 years, orange if > 10 years
- **Copies**: number of copies of the same title in the analysed set
- **Turnover rate**: progress bar — green (< 4), orange (4–8), red (≥ 8), grey (0)

**Filter panels** (toggle ⊟ top right):
- **CREW score distribution**: histogram by score value (0–5+) with range slider — filters items by score range
- Clickable histograms by medium type, genre, audience, condition
- Turnover rate histogram with min/max slider
- Publication year and acquisition year histograms with sliders

### Recommended weeding workflow

1. **Report → High Score (≥ 5)** — overview of urgent candidates
2. **Filter** by medium type if the collection is large
3. **Print** or note the barcodes of flagged candidates
4. **Physical inspection** of each flagged item (MUSTIE criteria)
5. **Decision**: remove / repair / order new copy
6. **Remove from system**: via the Inventory page → update condition or delete the item
7. **Stamp** removed items "WITHDRAWN" and set them aside physically
8. **Compensatory acquisition**: for each batch removed, plan purchases in the segments identified as popular (Most Borrowed report)

> **Tip:** The Most Borrowed and CREW reports work as a pair. Weeding frees up space and budget; the most borrowed titles reveal in-demand genres. Targeted purchases in those categories maximise collection usage.

## Never Borrowed Report

A CREW variant focused on items with **zero circulation**. Uses the same table and filters as the CREW report, plus a turnover rate histogram to filter by range.

Useful at the start of the year to identify last year's acquisitions that have never circulated.

## Overdue Report

Lists all loans past their due date, grouped by class.

For each overdue item:
- Student name and number (clickable to their record)
- Title and barcode
- Checkout date and due date
- Number of days overdue

> **Tip:** Filter by class and share the list with the class teacher for direct follow-up.

## Holds Report

Lists all active reservations with their current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🟦 **Waiting** | In the queue |
| 🟢 **Ready** | Item available — hand it to the student |
| 🔴 **Expired** | Not collected within the deadline |
| ⚪ **Cancelled / Fulfilled** | Hold is closed |

## Active Loans Report

Lists all items currently on loan, grouped by class.

The days-remaining badge shows:
- 🟢 **Green**: 4 days or more
- 🟠 **Orange**: 3 days or fewer
- 🔴 **Red**: overdue

## Common issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Overdue report is empty | No loans are currently overdue — that is good news! |
| A returned book still appears overdue | Check that the return was properly recorded in the Returns page. |
| CREW report shows hundreds of results | Start with "High Score (≥ 5)" + filter by medium type. Increase the minimum age. |
| A good book has a high CREW score | The score is a guide. If the book is popular, keep it or order a new copy. |
| An Éduscol list title has a high score | Éduscol reference titles are exempt from age criteria — check physical condition only. |
| Turnover rate < 1 for the whole collection | Weeding or collection realignment signal — check the Most Borrowed report to identify what works. |
| Turnover rate > 9 for a title | Order additional copies or similar titles. |
